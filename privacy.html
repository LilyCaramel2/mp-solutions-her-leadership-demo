Privacy & cookie notice

Draft for client approval. This notice is a practical starting point for 1000 Women Trust. It has not been approved by the Trust, its legal adviser, or its webmaster, and it must be reviewed before being treated as the organisation’s final privacy notice.

This draft applies to the event information page for HER Leadership – Our Future, hosted for review at mp-solutions-her-leadership-demo.vercel.app. The final publication address may be 1000women.co.za/her-event, subject to the Trust’s webmaster and domain process.

Information the page may handle

The page is designed to provide event information and send visitors to the Webtickets booking route or to the Trust’s enquiry email. If a visitor contacts the Trust by email, the Trust may receive the visitor’s name, contact details, message, booking enquiry, partnership enquiry, or other information the visitor chooses to provide.

The hosting platform may also process technical information such as an IP address, browser type, device information, request time, and server logs for security and service operation.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, process ticket or partnership requests, provide event information, maintain the page, protect the service, and meet applicable record-keeping or legal duties. The Trust should confirm its lawful basis, retention periods, authorised recipients, and any cross-border processing before approval.

Bookings and external services

The page links to Webtickets for ticket booking. Webtickets may collect and process information under its own terms and privacy notice. Visitors should review the relevant third-party notice before submitting booking information. The page also links to Trust websites and email addresses, which may have their own systems and notices.

Cookies and similar technologies

This static page is intended to operate without advertising cookies or an analytics profile. The current preview loads a styling script from Tailwind’s public CDN, and the hosting platform may create technical logs. If analytics, pixels, embedded media, forms, consent tools, or other third-party services are added, this notice must be revised and any required consent process must be implemented before use.

Data security and retention

The Trust should retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected or for a documented legal, accounting, safeguarding, or operational requirement. Access should be limited to people who need the information for the event or Trust’s work, with reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.

Visitor rights and contact

For questions about personal information, visitors should contact the Trust using the approved privacy contact supplied by 1000 Women Trust. The Trust should insert the correct privacy contact, postal address, information officer details, and complaint route before publishing this notice.
