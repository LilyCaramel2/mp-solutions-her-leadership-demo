The dialogue

Leadership becomes

South Africa’s women’s leadership conversation needs space for accountability, equality, justice, and collective action. HER Leadership – Our Future gathers people who are building that work across communities, institutions, sectors, and generations.

The day is shaped as a national leadership dialogue during 16 Days of Activism for No Violence Against Women and Children.
